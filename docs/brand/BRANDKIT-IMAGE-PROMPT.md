# Brand-kit image prompt (for /brandkit or any image model)

The vector logo in `logos/` is the source of truth. Use this prompt to generate a photographic presentation board; paste the real logo on top afterwards, since image models redraw letters badly.

```
Premium brand-guidelines board, 3 x 3 grid, 16:10, dark charcoal outer canvas (#1A1D18), even gutters, sparse typography.
Brand: The First Hospitality Group, a New York company that runs four high-class restaurants. Product: the staff operating app.
Strategy: executive, calm, quietly confident, never snobbish. Order behind a beautiful service.
Palette: white #FFFFFF primary, ink #111311 secondary, olive #5C6B2F as the single brand color. No gold, no beige, no gradients.
Logo: a square block monogram "TF" over "HG" in a clean geometric sans (Figtree Bold), white on olive. Leave the logo panel flat so the real vector can be placed.
Panels:
1. Logo cover on olive.
2. Monogram on a fine construction grid, ink on off-white.
3. Palette: three tall swatches white, ink, olive.
4. Pre-shift line-up in a dark-wood dining room, staff in black aprons holding phones, shallow depth of field.
5. Phone mockup: white app screen with an opening checklist, olive primary button.
6. Plated dish shot from above on a white plate, linen, natural light.
7. Bar station at close: bottles on a back bar, a tablet with an inventory count, low warm light.
8. Embroidered TFHG monogram on a black apron, close-up texture.
9. Name badge and menu card with the wordmark THE FIRST.
Mood: Manhattan fine dining, natural light, matte, subtle film grain, no neon, no emojis, no stock-photo smiles.
```
