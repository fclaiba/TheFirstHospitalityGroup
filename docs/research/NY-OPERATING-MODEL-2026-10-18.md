# New York fine-dining operating model: evidence and product implications

Researched 2026-10-18. This supplements the older role survey; current role and scope decisions remain in the blueprint. Public employer descriptions reveal responsibilities, not confidential operating manuals. No universal New York hierarchy is asserted.

## Primary sources

1. [Per Se: General Manager](https://thomaskeller.com/new-york-new-york/per-se/sandra-bohlsen/). The employer describes daily restaurant oversight, dining-room mentorship and a career through captain and maitre d'. Its team separates general management, chef de cuisine, pastry, beverage leadership and head sommelier. Product inference: managers must help people learn and verify execution, not merely acknowledge announcements.
2. [Restaurant DANIEL: team and operating description](https://www.danielnyc.com/about). Distinct culinary, pastry, venue management, wine, operations and private-dining leaders demonstrate functional specialization. Product inference: common company standards need area-specific execution and event staffing. Existing positions remain the agreed v1 model; pastry and events can have local titles rather than silently inventing new permission layers.
3. [Dinex corporate team](https://www.danielboulud.com/team). Operations directors coexist with corporate culinary, beverage and people functions. Product inference: company standards and venue execution require separate surfaces, with explicit delegation and traceable decisions.
4. [USHG careers](https://www.ushg.com/careers) and [company organization](https://www.ushg.com/about). Employer pages distinguish restaurant, leadership and home-office work and emphasize developing technical skills and hospitality behaviors. Product inference: instructions should be teachable and respectful, with practice feedback rather than public ranking.
5. [USHG Director of Training posting](https://www.hirebridge.com/jobseeker2/viewdetailintra.asp?acceptShift=0&cid=6687&come=IN&joblistid=595379&logo=yes&page=3&s=). The page is dated **2022-09-16**, not a verified current vacancy. Its description links curriculum to advancement, onboarding, managers and line staff, and practical demonstration criteria. It supports the training design historically; it is not evidence of a current staffing requirement.

These sources do not establish TFHG venue names, staffing ratios, exact schedules, specific house rules, certification criteria or a mandatory reporting ladder. Those are company inputs. The existing TFHG configured chain is retained. No legal rule is inferred from these sources.

## Macro process and accountability

| Phase | Accountable role | Execution and handoff | Digital evidence | Current surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Set standards | Owner, director | Culinary and area leaders contribute; venue GM implements | Published rules, menu and training versions; audit | Company setup, menu, rules, training setup |

| Review and improve | GM, director, owner | Read reasons, follow up with the responsible leader, update standards and coaching | Venue states, proposals, training evidence, audit | Venue, Company, Team |

## All-position operating contract

Every production position needs an outcome, preparation steps, service decisions, closing/handoff steps, permitted actions and configured supervisor. The blueprint's position table defines scope; the role playbook turns it into a navigable daily process. Practical demonstration is human-reviewed and does not automatically change job access or employment status.

## Gaps that evidence does not close

Live multi-device delivery, actual venue configuration, real content and photographs, owner-approved SOPs, moderated usability, load/restore exercises and production monitoring require separate verification. Cross-shift corrective work needs an accountable follow-up lifecycle beyond a free-text close note. External reservations, POS, payroll and purchasing remain outside the agreed v1 scope. Commercial value and demand require customer validation; neither a hierarchy study nor passing code tests can prove them.
